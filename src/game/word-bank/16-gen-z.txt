# Gen Z and social media
> This is Gen Z slang or internet culture.
TIKTOK | The app you have open right now, one scroll and two hours are gone. | 1
SELFIE | A photo of yourself, arm stretched out, best angle only. | 1
HASHTAG | The # symbol in front of a word on social media. | 1
EMOJI | Tiny pictures like 😂 and 🔥 you put in messages. | 1
MEME | A funny picture with text that everyone shares. | 1
FOLLOW | Tap this so you never miss my class again. | 1
SHARE | Send the video to your friend with "this is so you". | 1
LIKE | Tap the heart, it is free and makes people happy. | 1
VIRAL | When a video spreads everywhere in one day. | 2

INFLUENCER | Gets paid to post outfits and food on social media. | 2
STREAMER | Plays games live while thousands watch and chat. | 2

SLAY | When you look amazing or do something perfectly. | 2
